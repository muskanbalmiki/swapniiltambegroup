About

Swapniil Tambe is a dynamic entrepreneur, content creator, and fitness enthusiast who leads with passion and purpose. As Chairman of the Swapnil Tambe Group, he heads ventures like Planning Labs, Dezain Labs, Trishala Realty, Tarsh Enterprises, and Bandhan—each known for innovation, creativity, and impact. An expert in Experiential and Digital Marketing, Swapniil blends sharp business strategy with a creative mindset to build powerful brands and memorable experiences. His journey reflects resilience, vision, and a commitment to inspiring the next generation of leaders.

Early life

Swapniil Tambe was born into a Maharashtrian family in Mumbai, India. From a young age, he showed a keen interest in creativity, leadership, and business. He was raised in a middle-class environment that valued hard work and ambition. Inspired by his surroundings and fueled by curiosity, Swapniil grew up actively participating in school events and tech-based activities, gradually shaping a strong entrepreneurial mindset.

By the time he was in his teens, Swapniil began exploring content creation and the digital space. He took freelance gigs in marketing, learned design and branding, and soon started earning independently. With a natural talent for networking and strategy, he built early projects that laid the foundation for his future ventures. His experiences during these years instilled in him the values of consistency, creativity, and self-growth that he continues to uphold as a modern entrepreneur.

Companies

Planning Labs

Founded in 2012 by Swapniil Tambe, Planning Labs is an experiential and digital marketing agency known for turning concepts into impactful brand experiences. With a deep focus on execution and creativity, the company offers a wide range of services including BTL activations, corporate events, exhibitions, product launches, team-building activities, and more.

On the digital side, Planning Labs delivers social media management, paid advertising, content creation, influencer campaigns, and design solutions that drive engagement and visibility. By blending on-ground expertise with digital innovation, Planning Labs helps brands create memorable moments and meaningful connections.

Dezain Labs

Launched in 2025 by Swapniil Tambe, Dezain Labs is an interior design studio focused on innovative space-saving solutions. Specializing in maximizing functionality in compact homes and offices, the firm blends modern aesthetics with smart utility. Known for their tailored designs and intelligent layouts, Dezain Labs is redefining how urban spaces are utilized without compromising on style or comfort.

Tarsh Enterprises

Founded by Swapniil Tambe in 2022, Tarsha Enterprises operates across multiple sectors including medical equipment supply, trading, market research, import-export, and bulk procurement. With a diverse investment portfolio, the company serves as a dynamic business arm within ST Group. Its multifaceted operations and strategic sourcing make it a reliable partner for large-scale supply and international trade ventures.

Trishala Realty

Founded by Swapniil Tambe, Trishala Realty is a real estate consultancy specializing in commercial property sales, home loan assistance, and investment advisory services. With a client-first approach, the firm simplifies the home-buying journey by combining property expertise with financial guidance. Trishala Realty bridges the gap between real estate and smart investing, empowering individuals and businesses to make informed property decisions.

Bandhan

Bandhan specializes in cross-culture weddings, creating beautifully blended celebrations where diverse traditions, rituals, and families come together in harmony. We understand that love knows no boundaries—and neither should your wedding. With expertise in handling multi-cultural ceremonies, our team designs every detail with cultural sensitivity and artistic finesse. From an Indian-South African celebration to a Maharashtrian-Bengali fusion, Bandhan ensures each wedding reflects the unique story of the couple.
